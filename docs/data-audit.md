# Phase 0 Data Audit

As of 2026-11-14. Generated by `python -m procurement_risk audit`; numbers come from the audit sample in `data/raw`.

## Decisions

- **Primary target: has_value_addition_text_recovered** — hidden share 70.8% > 30% and text matcher precision 84% >= 80%: label = explicit OR text-matched amendments.
- **Secondary target:** has_time_extension (`dias_adicionados > 0`).
- **`valor_del_contrato`:** excluded.
- **Closed contract:** current end date at least 180 days before the as-of date. Only closed contracts are labelled and used for training/evaluation. `estado_contrato` is not used: in states `Cerrado`, `En ejecución`, `terminado`, `Modificado`, `Aprobado`, `cedido`, `Suspendido`, `enviado Proveedor` at least 90% of contracts ended more than 180 days ago, so the field is not kept up to date.
